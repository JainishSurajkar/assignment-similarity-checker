Before mining a dataset, raw records need to be prepared for analysis. A
useful preparation workflow removes repeated records, deals with absent
values, scales numerical features, and represents categories numerically.
Transformations must be fitted using the training portion so information from
the test portion does not leak into the experiment.

Text needs a similar preparation step. We clean line endings and excess spaces
but retain empty lines as paragraph separators. Each paragraph is then
represented by TF-IDF weights. Terms appearing everywhere carry less
importance, while distinctive terms help separate one document from another.

An honest experiment keeps a final set of examples aside until the model is
finished. Results on a small artificial collection are useful for a classroom
demonstration, but they are not evidence about actual student submissions.
