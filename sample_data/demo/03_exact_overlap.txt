Machine learning is a method in which a computer learns patterns from
examples instead of receiving every rule explicitly. During training, the
model studies data and adjusts its internal parameters. After training, it
uses the learned patterns to make predictions about new observations.

The quality of a machine learning system depends strongly on the quality and
representativeness of its training data. Missing values, incorrect labels, or
biased examples can lead to unreliable predictions even when the algorithm
itself is well designed.
