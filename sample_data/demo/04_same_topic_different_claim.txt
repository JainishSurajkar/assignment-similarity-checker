Machine learning is used in education to support several activities, such as
recommending practice questions and identifying students who may need help.
These systems can save teachers time by summarizing patterns in student
responses.

Teachers should not treat a prediction as a final academic decision. Human
review is necessary because a student's performance may be affected by
circumstances that are not represented in the available data.
Cosine similarity compares the angle between two vectors rather than their
length. In a text mining system, TF-IDF gives a document a weighted coordinate
for each term. Two assignments about the same topic can therefore receive a
moderate score even when they explain different ideas and use different
examples.

An evaluation should compare predictions with labelled pairs. Precision
describes how many predicted matches are relevant, while recall describes how
many relevant pairs were found. A validation split can select a threshold,
but the test split should remain untouched until the final measurement.

These concepts are related to the demonstration application, but a score is
not a probability that copying occurred. A faculty reviewer must consider
prompts, references, standard terminology, and the surrounding context.
